# 🎤 Opening Pitch — Executive Meeting (Jelcos AI ↔ Emergent)

> **Target length: 2 minutes.** ~290 words at 145 wpm.
> Practice it 2-3 times out loud before the call. Cut from end if running long.

---

## ✅ The pitch (read aloud, conversational tone)

> Thank you for taking the time. I'll be quick and direct.
>
> I'm building **Jelcos AI** — a decision-intelligence platform with 32 modules and 89 features,
> live in production on AWS EC2 plus Cloudflare plus MongoDB Atlas, with a self-serve admin
> regression suite, a tier-matrix subscription model, and an Indian-market focus. Every line
> of code in it was shipped through Emergent. So I'm not just a user — I'm a **case study**
> for what's possible on your platform.
>
> I'm here because I want to keep building this way. But I need three things to keep doing so
> productively.
>
> **First** — I've burned a significant chunk of credits not on building features, but on
> the agent fixing bugs *it introduced or carried forward from prior sessions*. Phantom
> version pins that broke my EC2 deploy twice. A React hydration bug that hid 80% of my
> modules from real users for weeks. I have the evidence quantified. I'd like to discuss
> a goodwill credit waiver for those specific incidents.
>
> **Second** — I'm hitting the LLM budget cap on the universal key roughly nine times across
> my forks. My AI features silently return 503 to my paying customers. I'd like to discuss
> a higher tier, or a "bring-your-own-key" option, so I can budget this myself.
>
> **Third** — every time I fork, the next agent loses context and I have to re-explain my
> branch, my EC2 path, my deployment steps. I'd like to discuss a persistent project memory,
> and ideally a direct line to one Emergent engineer who knows my project.
>
> In return, I'll be a design partner. I'll write the case study. I'll refer founders.
>
> I have a one-page brief, a CSV with the burn-audit numbers, and three pain stories with
> screenshots ready. Where would you like to start?

## 🎯 Delivery tips

| Tip | Why |
|---|---|
| **Don't read it verbatim.** Internalize the 3 asks. | Reading sounds rehearsed; speaking sounds confident. |
| **Slow down at the 3 numbered asks.** | These are the only sentences they'll remember. |
| **Don't apologize for asking.** | You've spent money. They want to keep you. Be matter-of-fact. |
| **End with a question, not a statement.** | "Where would you like to start?" hands them the wheel — they'll feel respected. |
| **If they push back on any ask, don't negotiate immediately.** Say "Let me hear your perspective first." | Buys you reasoning time; you learn their constraints. |
| **Keep your laptop closed for the first 2 minutes.** | Eye contact > slides. The brief is for AFTER the pitch. |

## 🧠 If they ask follow-ups, here are sharp 1-liner answers

| Q | A |
|---|---|
| "How much in credit refund are you asking for?" | "About X credits — calculated as the spend on the 8 agent-introduced bugs listed in my brief. Happy to walk through line by line." (Fill X from CSV) |
| "What if we can't offer a higher LLM tier?" | "Then a BYO-key option would be equally valuable to me — and I'd guess to other production customers too." |
| "What does 'design partner' mean to you?" | "I'll give you weekly feedback on fork-context retention, run beta features early, and write a case study at GA." |
| "What's your timeline pressure?" | "Production users are on the app daily. Every fork that loses 30 minutes is a real user-facing slowdown for me." |
| "Why not just switch to another platform?" | "Because I've invested deeply in Emergent's tooling, and I'd rather make this work than start over. That's why I'm here." (← Powerful close.) |

## ⛔ Things to AVOID saying

- ❌ "Your agent is buggy" → use **"agent-introduced regressions"** instead. Less accusatory.
- ❌ "I want a refund" → use **"goodwill credit waiver for X documented incidents"**.
- ❌ "Your platform is limiting" → use **"current platform ceiling"** and frame as roadmap.
- ❌ "I might leave" — unless you genuinely will. Empty threats are read in 2 seconds.
- ❌ Long monologues. Pause and let them speak. **3 asks. 3 pauses. Listen.**
